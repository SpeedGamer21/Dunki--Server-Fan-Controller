Instrument cluster

Each needle shows the PWM output sent to the PWM-to-voltage module. The yellow marker is the curve's target speed, and the red zone starts at 85%. Hit START/STOP to switch a fan on or off.

Fan curve tune

Drag the points to tune the map. X = temperature °C, Y = PWM command %. The dashed line marks the current temperature.
